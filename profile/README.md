We build software for clinical research sites and for anyone whose documents have to hold up later. Every product keeps a record you can show an auditor, and none of them asks you to trust a black box.

### Products

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[Nexrial](https://nexrial.com)** | Clinical trial management for independent research sites. Studies, subjects, visits and regulatory binders in one place, with an append-only audit trail and e-signatures designed and tested against 21 CFR Part 11. |
| **[PurpleSign](https://purplesign.app)** | Get it signed. Keep the proof. Signers finish on any phone without an account, and every signed copy comes with a certificate. |
| **[QuillPDF](https://quillpdf.com)** | Free PDF tools that run entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your machine. |
| **[ICH-GCP SOPs](https://purpledirective.com/sop-bundle/)** | Standard operating procedure templates for clinical research sites, ready to adopt. |

### Open source

| Project | What it does |
